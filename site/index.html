Privacy policy

Lumière collects no personal data

Android app com.wynov.lumiere · Publisher: Wynov · Last updated November 2, 2026

Lumière works only on your local network. The app has no server, creates no account, and sends no data to Wynov or to any third party. What you save stays on your phone.

1. Data we collect

None. Wynov does not receive, store or share any data about you. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or social network SDK.

2. Data stored on your phone

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage, on the device only:

  • your list of lamps: name, room, model, local IP address, MAC address and device ID;
  • your custom scenes and recent colors;
  • the last lamp you viewed.

This information never leaves your phone. You can remove a lamp in Settings, and erase everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

3. Wi‑Fi password

To add a new lamp, you enter your Wi‑Fi password. The app sends it directly to the lamp, over the lamp's temporary access point, so the lamp can join your network. The app does not keep it and sends it nowhere else.

4. Network communications

All communication happens on your local network, between your phone and your lamps:

PurposeProtocolRecipient
Adding a new lampUDP 54321 (miIO)The lamp, on its access point
Finding lampsSSDP and local network scanDevices on your network
Commands (power, color…)TCP 55443The lamp

The app does not contact any server on the internet, including Yeelight's or Xiaomi's.

5. Android permissions

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in Android settings. Without them, you won't be able to add new lamps.

6. Sharing with third parties

None. No data is sold, rented or transferred.

7. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Your rights

Since Wynov holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Local data is entirely under your control (see section 2). For any GDPR question, contact us as shown below.

Help and contact

Answers to the most common questions, then the address to reach us.

Before you start

  • Your phone and the lamp must be on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • The lamp only connects over 2.4 GHz. If your router splits 2.4 and 5 GHz, pick the 2.4 GHz network.
  • WPA2-secured networks only. Enterprise networks and networks with a login page (hotels, public Wi‑Fi) are not supported.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new lamp?
  1. In Lumière, tap +.
  2. A new lamp is already in pairing mode. Otherwise, reset it: at the switch, turn it off and on 5 times in a row, about 2 seconds each time. The lamp changes color, then stays white.
  3. Pick the detected lamp (a “yeelink‑light‑…” network) and accept Android's prompt to join that network.
  4. Choose your Wi‑Fi and enter its password. It takes about a minute; the lamp blinks when it's done.
My lamp is already set up with the Yeelight app

In the Yeelight app, open the lamp's settings and turn on “LAN Control”. The lamp then shows up under “My lamps” in Lumière, with an “Add” button. No reset needed.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Android requires this permission to list nearby Wi‑Fi networks and read the current network name. Lumière does not read your location and sends nothing. Details in the privacy policy.

The lamp shows as “offline”

Check that your phone is on your home Wi‑Fi (not on mobile data). If your router gave the lamp a new address, the app finds it again on its own within a few seconds. Otherwise, switch the lamp off for 10 seconds and back on.

Can I control my lamps when I'm away from home?

No. That's the trade-off of working without the cloud: commands only travel over your local network.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android Settings › Apps › Lumière › Storage. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
